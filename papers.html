Past papers and grade guide

Official question papers to sit under exam conditions, and the grade your marks reach.

Every official 4CP0 question paper from 2019 to 2025. Sit a full pair under timed conditions: Paper 1 (written, 2 hours) and Paper 2 (practical, 3 hours, on a computer with the code files and Python). Then give it to your teacher to mark against the official mark scheme.

Official mark schemes are for teachers only. Students check individual parts with “Check my answer” on each question page, and check Paper 2 code with “Run the checks” on each coding page.

Grade guide

After a full pair has been marked against the official scheme, enter the two marks to see the grade they reach on the official Pearson June 2026 boundaries for 4CP0 (out of 160). The guide also shows the grade that stays secure with a 10-mark teacher buffer, an internal caution margin, not a Pearson boundary.
