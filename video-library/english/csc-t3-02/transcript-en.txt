On a cold day the temperature might be minus five degrees. On a warm day, plus twenty. A number like this has two parts: a sign, plus or minus, and a size. Sign and magnitude stores exactly those two parts. The leftmost bit is the sign bit: zero means plus, one means minus. The other seven bits hold the magnitude, the size, using sixty-four down to one. For minus five, keep the same seven magnitude bits and change only the sign bit to one. So one zero zero zero, zero one zero one is minus five. The largest magnitude is seven ones, which is one hundred and twenty-seven. So the range is minus one hundred and twenty-seven up to plus one hundred and twenty-seven. Zero has two patterns: plus zero and minus zero.

Pattern to denary: separate the first bit from the other seven. Read the sign from the first bit. Then add the place values above the ones in the other seven bits. For example, one one zero one, zero zero zero one. The sign bit is one, so it is negative. The size is sixty-four plus sixteen plus one, which is eighty-one. So the answer is minus eighty-one. Denary to pattern: ignore the minus sign and convert the size to seven bits. Then put one in front for a negative number, or zero for a positive one.

Here is a real question from Paper 1 in June 2022. Convert the denary number minus forty-three to eight-bit binary, using sign and magnitude. It is worth two marks. The number is negative, so the sign bit is one. Now the size, forty-three. Sixty-four does not fit, so zero. Thirty-two fits: write one, and eleven is left. Sixteen does not fit: zero. Eight fits: write one, and three is left. Four does not fit: zero. Two fits: write one, and one is left. One fits: write one, and nothing is left. The answer is one zero one zero, one zero one one. Check: sign one means minus, and thirty-two plus eight plus two plus one is forty-three. One mark is for the sign bit, and one for the seven magnitude bits. A common mistake is counting the sign bit as one hundred and twenty-eight. Then one zero one zero, one zero one one reads as one hundred and seventy-one. In sign and magnitude it means minus forty-three. Another mistake is giving minus one hundred and twenty-eight as the lowest value. That is the two's complement limit. In sign and magnitude, the lowest is minus one hundred and twenty-seven.

Now you try. First, convert minus one hundred to eight-bit sign and magnitude. Second, what denary number does zero zero one one, zero one one zero represent in sign and magnitude? Pause here and work it out.

Part a. The sign bit is one. Then one hundred: sixty-four fits, leaving thirty-six. Thirty-two fits, leaving four. Sixteen and eight do not fit. Four fits, leaving zero. Two and one are zero. The answer is one one one zero, zero one zero zero. Part b. The sign bit is zero, so it is positive. The size is thirty-two plus sixteen plus four plus two, which is fifty-four. So the answer is plus fifty-four. Remember the method: the first bit is the sign, the other seven bits are the size, and the range is minus one hundred and twenty-seven to plus one hundred and twenty-seven.
