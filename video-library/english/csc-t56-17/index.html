Read the narration

A light switch is either on or off. Normal computers work like that, storing data as bits that are zero or one. New kinds of computing change this rule, or do not use silicon chips at all. You need four emerging technologies: quantum computing, D N A computing, artificial intelligence, and nanotechnology. Quantum computers store data in qubits, made from sub-atomic particles such as electrons. Superposition means a qubit can be zero and one at the same time. Entanglement links pairs of qubits, so one affects the other. D N A computing uses strands of D N A and chemical reactions, instead of silicon chips and electricity. It uses a four-letter code, A, C, G and T, and can run huge numbers of operations in parallel. Artificial intelligence is software that learns patterns from large sets of training data, then makes predictions about new data. It is used in medical diagnosis, chatbots, face recognition and self-driving vehicles. Nanotechnology is engineering with atoms and molecules, at about one to one hundred nanometres. It is used for tiny transistors, scratch-resistant coatings, and medicines delivered to exact places in the body.

Identify the technology and recall its key idea. For a difference, give what the new technology uses, whereas what traditional computing uses. And never repeat what the question already tells you.

Here is a real question from Paper 1 in June 2025. The question tells you quantum computers use superposition. Give two other characteristics of quantum computers. It is worth two marks. Superposition is in the question, so cross it out. That leaves qubits and entanglement. One: data is stored in qubits, made from sub-atomic particles such as electrons or photons. Two: pairs of qubits can be entangled, so the state of one affects the other. This explanation is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is calling nanotechnology just very small technology. Mention atoms and molecules, or the nanometre scale. Another mistake is saying quantum computers are just faster. Give a feature: qubits, superposition or entanglement.

Now you try. A hospital uses software trained on thousands of scans to spot signs of illness. Name this technology, and give one ethical issue. Pause here and work it out.

It is artificial intelligence, because it learns from training data. One ethical issue: if the scans came mostly from one group of people, it could be less accurate for others, which is unfair.
