Idea. Julius Caesar sent secret messages by moving every letter a few places along the alphabet. With a shift of plus three, A becomes D, B becomes E, and C becomes F. This is the Caesar cipher. The alphabet wraps round. After Z comes A again. So with a shift of plus three, X becomes A, Y becomes B and Z becomes C. A positive shift moves forward. A negative shift moves back. To decrypt, apply the opposite shift. Two shifts in a row add together, so minus six then plus eight is the same as plus two.

Method. To encrypt, move each letter by the shift, wrapping past Z or A. It helps to count on your fingers, one step at a time. If counting is slow, number the letters, with A as nought and Z as twenty-five. Add the shift. If the answer is more than twenty-five, take away twenty-six. For example, Y is twenty-four, plus three is twenty-seven, take away twenty-six is one, which is B. To find an unknown shift, compare one plaintext letter with its ciphertext letter and count the gap. Then check the gap with the other letters.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper 2 in June 2023. Encrypt PIXEL with a shift of minus four. Then find the shift that turns CLOUD into F O R X G. It is worth two marks. Minus four means move back four places. P goes back to L. I goes back to E. X goes back to T. E goes back to A. L goes back to H. For row two, pair C with F. C, D, E, F is three places forward. Check the others: L to O, O to R, U to X, D to G. All are plus three. This explanation is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is forgetting to wrap round. With plus three, Y does not go off the end. It wraps to B. Another mistake is calling the cipher weak without saying why. There are only twenty-five useful shifts, so an attacker can try them all very quickly. That is a brute-force attack.

Your turn. Now you try. Encrypt the word ZEBRA with a Caesar shift of plus five. Watch the Z. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. Z wraps round: A, B, C, D, E, so Z becomes E. E becomes J. B becomes G. R becomes W. A becomes F. The answer is E J G W F. Remember the method: move every letter by the same shift, wrap round past Z or A, and give the sign with any shift. Now try the practice questions for this type.
