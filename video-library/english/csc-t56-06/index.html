Read the narration

Look at the top of a phone screen. It might show four G or five G. That tells you which generation of mobile network it is using. Three G, four G and five G are generations of mobile standards, and all of them are wireless. Two words matter. Bandwidth is how much data can be sent each second. Latency is the delay before a response comes back. Higher bandwidth and lower latency are better. Each newer generation has higher bandwidth and lower latency. Five G can also connect many more devices in the same area, which suits the Internet of Things, everyday devices that connect to the internet. Why is five G faster? It uses new, higher radio frequency bands, where much wider channels are available. Wider channels carry more data each second. But higher frequencies travel shorter distances. So five G needs more masts, placed closer together.

Recall the three improvements: higher bandwidth, lower latency, more devices. For give, write two different ones. For explain, add what the improvement means for the person.

Here is a real question from Paper 1 in November 2021. Explain one benefit to Carlo of using five G rather than three G. It is worth two marks. Pick a property: five G has much higher bandwidth and lower latency than three G. Then ask what that does for Carlo. Data moves faster, with less delay. Now join it with so. Five G has higher bandwidth and lower latency, so Carlo can share data in far less time and gets quicker responses in his calls and apps. This explanation is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is just saying five G is better, or faster. Name the feature, and say the consequence. Another mistake is saying five G has a longer range. Higher frequencies travel less far, which is why more masts are needed.

Now you try. A city connects thousands of smart bins and streetlights to the internet. Explain one reason five G suits this better than four G. Pause here and work it out.

Five G can connect many more devices in the same area. So thousands of bins and streetlights can all stay connected at once, without the network becoming overloaded. Remember the method: name the property, add so, then give the benefit to the person. Now try the practice questions for this type.
