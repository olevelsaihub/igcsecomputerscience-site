You save a photo to an online drive on your phone, then open it on a laptop. Where was it really? Not on your phone, but on a big computer somewhere else, called a server. That is cloud storage. Files are saved on remote servers, owned and managed by another company, a third party, and reached over the internet. Benefits: access from anywhere on any device, easy sharing, storage that can grow or shrink, and backups kept by the provider. Drawbacks: you need a reliable connection, you rely on the provider, and you keep paying a subscription. There are security risks too. Hackers may target the provider. Dishonest staff could misuse files. Files could be intercepted while they upload or download. So security is shared. The user chooses a strong password and keeps it private. The provider secures its servers, encrypts the data, updates its security software and keeps backups.

Start from the definition. Decide what the question wants: a meaning, a benefit, a drawback or security. Give a point plus a linked reason. For a discuss question, cover both sides, then judge.

Here is a real question from Paper 1 in June 2025. Describe what is meant by cloud storage. It is worth two marks. Ask three questions. Where are the files? On remote servers. Who looks after them? A third-party provider. How do you reach them? Over the internet. So the answer is: storage space on remote servers, owned and managed by a third party. An internet connection is needed to reach the files. This explanation is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is saying files are stored on the internet, or in the sky. They are stored on remote servers. The internet is only how you reach them. Another mistake is giving the provider a user's duty. The user chooses the password. The provider secures the servers and encrypts the data.

Now you try. A music producer records songs at a studio and edits them at home. Explain one benefit and one drawback of saving the songs in cloud storage. Pause here and work it out.

Benefit: the songs can be opened at the studio and at home, so there is no drive to carry. Drawback: with no internet connection, the songs cannot be opened, and large files may upload slowly.
