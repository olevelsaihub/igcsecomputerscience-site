Knowledge card

Mobile generations: 3G, 4G and 5G

Written

Each newer mobile generation gives higher bandwidth, lower latency, and more connected devices. 5G uses higher frequencies, so each mast reaches less far.

  • 3G, 4G and 5G are generations of mobile (cellular) communication standards; all of them are wireless.
  • Each newer generation gives higher bandwidth, meaning more data can be sent each second.
  • Each newer generation gives lower latency, meaning less delay between sending a request and getting a response.
  • 5G can connect many more devices in the same area, which suits the Internet of Things (everyday devices connected to the internet).
  • 5G uses new, higher radio frequency bands where much wider channels are available; wider channels carry more data each second, which gives 5G its higher bandwidth.
  • Higher frequencies travel shorter distances, so 5G needs more masts placed closer together.
  • For the user, these features mean faster downloads and streaming, quicker responses, and a more reliable connection in busy places.

  • Bandwidth and latency.
  • Radio waves and frequency (waves per second).

The method

  1. Recall three gains

    Bandwidth, latency, more devices.

  2. Give

    Two different improvements.

  3. Explain

    Add the benefit to the person.

  4. Multiple choice

    Reject slower, more latency, less bandwidth.

  5. Keep them apart

    Bandwidth is amount; latency is delay.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Just 'faster'

    Say bandwidth or latency.

  • ✗
    Mixing the two

    Amount of data is not delay.

  • ✗
    Longer range

    5G masts reach less far.

Worked explanation

  1. Pick one property of 5G compared with 3G: much higher bandwidth and lower latency.
  2. Ask: what does that do for Carlo? Data moves faster with less delay.
  3. Write the property, then 'so', then the benefit to Carlo.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

5G has much higher bandwidth and lower latency than 3G, so Carlo can download, upload and share data in far less time and gets quicker responses during his calls and apps.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the 5G improvement (faster, quicker-responding connection). One mark for the linked benefit to Carlo (moving or sharing data in less time).
