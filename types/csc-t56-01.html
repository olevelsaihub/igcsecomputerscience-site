Knowledge card

Network types: LAN, WAN, PAN and why we network

Written

Match the distance to the network: PAN around one person, LAN one building or site, WAN many sites. Compared with a WAN, a LAN has higher bandwidth and lower latency.

  • A network is two or more computers or devices linked together so they can share data and resources.
  • Common reasons to network computers: share files, share hardware such as printers, share one internet connection, and let people communicate and work together.
  • Networks also let an organisation manage computers from one place: central backups, central software updates and central security settings.
  • A LAN (local area network) covers a small area such as one building or one site, and the organisation usually owns all of its equipment.
  • A WAN (wide area network) covers a large geographical area and joins separate LANs together, usually over links rented from telecoms companies; the internet is the biggest WAN.
  • A PAN (personal area network) connects devices around one person, for example a phone linked to earbuds by Bluetooth or a phone hotspot shared with a laptop.
  • Compared with a WAN, a LAN usually has higher bandwidth (more bits per second) and lower latency (less delay).
  • In the client-server model, a central server provides services such as file storage or logins, and the client computers ask it for them.
  • In the peer-to-peer model, every device is equal and shares directly with the others, with no central server.

The method

  1. Find the distance

    One person, one site, or many sites.

  2. Name the type

    One correct name only.

  3. Usage model

    Central server, or equal devices?

  4. Reasons

    Skip any reason the question gave.

  5. Differences

    WAN ..., whereas LAN ..., one feature.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Vague 'slower'

    Say bandwidth or latency.

  • ✗
    Same point twice

    Two area points count once.

  • ✗
    Type or model

    LAN is a type; client-server is a model.

What a complete response includes

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Pick a feature to compare. Start with size of area.
  2. Write the WAN side and the LAN side in one sentence joined by 'whereas'.
  3. Pick a second, different feature: latency (delay).
  4. Write the WAN side and the LAN side again, and add a short reason.
  5. Check that the two lines are about different features.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1. A WAN spreads over a large geographical area, such as several cities or countries, whereas a LAN covers only a small area such as one building or site. 2. A WAN usually has higher latency (longer delays) than a LAN, because data must travel much further and pass through many more links.

How the marks are earned

Line 1 earns one mark for the area contrast. Line 2 earns one mark for the latency contrast. Each line states both networks on the same feature, and the two features are different.
