Knowledge card

Connecting to the internet: switch, WAP, router and modem

Written

Wired devices plug into the switch; wireless devices join the WAP, which is cabled to the switch. Then router, modem, ISP, internet: always in that order.

  • A switch connects wired devices in a LAN and sends each packet only to the device it is meant for, using MAC addresses.
  • A wireless access point (WAP) lets wireless devices join the wired network; it is connected to the switch (or router) by a cable.
  • A router forwards packets between different networks, such as the home or office LAN and the internet.
  • A modem converts the digital data from the network into signals that can travel over the ISP's line, and converts incoming signals back.
  • The ISP (internet service provider) connects the modem to the rest of the internet.
  • The order from a device out to the internet is: device, switch, router, modem, ISP, internet.
  • Wired devices plug into the switch; wireless devices connect by radio to the WAP, and the WAP is cabled to the switch.
  • A home 'router' box usually combines a switch, a WAP, a router and a modem in one case.

4

The method

  1. Wired or wireless?

    Mark each end device.

  2. Wired links

    One line from each to the switch.

  3. Wireless link

    Dashed to the WAP, then WAP to switch.

  4. Go outwards

    Switch, router, modem, ISP, internet.

  5. Check

    Remove any extra lines.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Laptop to switch

    Wireless devices use the WAP.

  • ✗
    Order reversed

    Modem is near the ISP.

  • ✗
    Extra lines

    They cancel marks.

Worked explanation

  1. Wired devices: the two desktop computers. Wireless device: the laptop.
  2. Draw a solid line from each desktop to the switch.
  3. Draw a solid line from the wireless access point to the switch.
  4. Draw a dashed line from the laptop to the wireless access point.
  5. Check: four lines in total and no other lines.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Solid line from desktop computer 1 to the switch. Solid line from desktop computer 2 to the switch. Solid line from the wireless access point to the switch. Dashed line from the laptop computer to the wireless access point. No other lines are drawn.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the WAP-to-switch solid line, one mark for both desktop-to-switch solid lines, and one mark for the laptop-to-WAP dashed line.
