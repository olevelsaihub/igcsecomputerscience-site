Knowledge card

Network protocols: what they are and which one does what

Written

A protocol is a set of agreed rules. Match the task to the protocol: SMTP sends email, IMAP keeps devices in step, POP3 downloads, HTTP/HTTPS web pages, FTP files.

  • A protocol is a set of agreed rules that lets devices communicate, covering things like the format of data, its timing and how errors are checked.
  • Without shared protocols, devices made by different companies could not understand each other.
  • Ethernet is a family of protocols for wired LANs; it sets the cable and connector types, the speeds and how packets are checked for errors, and it works at the data link layer.
  • Wi-Fi is a family of protocols for wireless LANs that use radio waves.
  • HTTP (HyperText Transfer Protocol) moves web pages from a web server to a browser; HTTPS is the secure version that encrypts the data.
  • FTP (File Transfer Protocol) is used to move files between computers over a network.
  • SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) is used to send email.
  • POP3 (Post Office Protocol 3) downloads email to one device and usually removes it from the server.
  • IMAP (Internet Message Access Protocol) keeps email on the server and keeps every device in step with it.

Worked explanation

  1. Read the scenario: Isra reads email on a tablet and a smartphone.
  2. Two devices that must show the same mailbox means the mail should stay on the server: choose IMAP.
  3. Justify: say what IMAP does (keeps mail on the server, keeps devices synchronised).
  4. Link it to Isra: both devices show the same messages, and her devices do not need to store all her mail.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Email protocol: IMAP. Justification: IMAP keeps her emails stored on the mail server and keeps each device synchronised with it. So a message she reads, sends or deletes on her tablet shows the same way on her smartphone straight away, and her devices do not have to hold every email in their own limited storage.

How the marks are earned

One mark for naming IMAP. Two more marks for the justification: one for what IMAP does (mail stays on the server and is synchronised) and one for the link to Isra (both devices show the same mailbox, or her devices need little storage).
