Knowledge card

Cloud storage: what it is, pros, cons and security

Written

Cloud storage is files kept on remote servers run by a provider and reached over the internet. Every benefit or drawback needs a reason, and security is shared.

  • Cloud storage means saving files on remote servers owned by another company and reaching them over the internet.
  • You need an internet connection to upload or open files stored in the cloud.
  • Benefits include access from any place and any device, easy sharing and working together on files, and storage that can be scaled up or down.
  • Cloud providers usually keep backups, so files are not lost if your own device is lost or broken.
  • Drawbacks include needing a fast, reliable connection, relying on the provider, and ongoing subscription costs.
  • Security risks include hackers targeting the provider, dishonest staff at the provider, and files being intercepted during upload or download.
  • Security is shared: the user must choose strong passwords and keep them private; the provider must secure its servers, encrypt data, update security software and keep backups.
  • A discuss question needs benefits and drawbacks, linked to reasons, and ideally a short conclusion.

The method

  1. Start with the definition

    Remote servers, third party, internet.

  2. Read the command word

    Meaning, benefit, drawback or duty.

  3. Point plus reason

    Add a linked result for the person.

  4. Split duties

    User: password. Provider: servers, encryption.

  5. Discuss

    Both sides, then a short judgement.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    In the sky

    Say remote servers, not the sky.

  • ✗
    Duties swapped

    The user chooses a password, not the provider.

  • ✗
    One side only

    A discuss answer needs benefits and drawbacks.

What a complete response includes

Short answers credit each correct idea; explanations need a point plus a linked reason. The six-mark discussion is marked by levels: linked, balanced reasoning on benefits and drawbacks reaches the top level, while isolated points stay in the lowest level.

Worked explanation

  1. Ask: where are the files kept? On servers somewhere else, not on your own device.
  2. Ask: who looks after those servers? Another company, the provider.
  3. Ask: how do you reach the files? Over the internet.
  4. Write two separate facts.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Cloud storage is storage space on remote servers that is owned and managed by a third-party provider. You need an internet connection to access the files.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each separate fact up to two: stored remotely, managed by a third party, and needing an internet connection to access.

June 2023 · Paper 1 · Q6(d)

Answer plan

  1. Write one sentence defining cloud storage; optionally one on why it has grown.
  2. Benefit 1: access from anywhere and sharing or collaboration with permissions; explain how it helps the programmers.
  3. Benefit 2: lower cost and scalable space, plus automatic backups; explain what problem each removes.
  4. Drawback 1: needs a reliable internet connection and depends on the provider staying available.
  5. Drawback 2: security, privacy and legal control of data held by another company; mention how encryption reduces the risk.
  6. Conclude with a judgement (do benefits outweigh drawbacks?) and the conditions that make it true.
