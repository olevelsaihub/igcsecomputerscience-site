Knowledge card

Logical and arithmetic binary shifts

Calculation

A shift moves every bit left or right and drops the bits that fall off. Logical shifts fill with 0. An arithmetic right shift fills with copies of the sign bit.

  • A binary shift moves every bit left or right by a given number of places.
  • Bits that move off the end are lost.
  • In a logical shift, the empty places are always filled with 0s.
  • In an arithmetic right shift, the empty places on the left are filled with copies of the sign bit (the leftmost bit), so a negative number stays negative.
  • An arithmetic left shift fills the empty places on the right with 0s, like a logical left shift.
  • Each shift of one place left multiplies an unsigned number by 2; each shift right divides it by 2 (any remainder is lost).
  • Shifting right by 2 divides by 4; shifting right by 3 divides by 8.
  • Pearson's papers describe an arithmetic shift as one that keeps the sign of the number: on a right shift the sign bit is copied into the empty places; answer in those terms.

The calculation

Shift left n places = × 2ⁿ; shift right n places = ÷ 2ⁿ

Logical: fill with **0**. Arithmetic right: copy the **sign bit**.

  • Bits that fall off the end are lost; the length stays the same.
  • For a negative number, an arithmetic right shift fills with 1s.

Try itArithmetic right shift of 1100 0101 by 3 places.

  1. The sign bit is 1.
  2. Move 3 places right: the last three bits 101 fall off.
  3. Fill 3 places on the left with the sign bit: 111.
  4. 1111 1000

The method

  1. Note three facts

    Direction, places, logical or arithmetic.

  2. Move the bits

    Drop what falls off the end.

  3. Fill the gaps

    0s, or the sign bit for an arithmetic right shift.

  4. Count the bits

    The length must stay the same.

  5. Describe

    Give direction and number of places.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    0s after arithmetic right

    A negative number must stay negative.

  • ✗
    Wrong direction

    Left multiplies; right divides.

  • ✗
    Length changed

    Bits drop off; the pattern stays 8 long.

Worked explanation

  1. Pattern: 1100 0101. Shift: arithmetic, right, 3 places.
  2. The sign bit (leftmost) is 1.
  3. Move all bits 3 places right: the last three bits 101 fall off the end, leaving 11000 in the right-hand five places.
  4. Fill the three empty places on the left with copies of the sign bit: 111.
  5. Result: 111 then 11000, which is 1111 1000.
