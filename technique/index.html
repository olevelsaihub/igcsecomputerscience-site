Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Computer Science · 4CP0

Answer technique cards

How each command word is marked, with a method and an authored worked illustration.

01

Multiple choice (1 mark)

Choose the single correct option, usually from four labelled A to D, and mark it by putting a cross in its box. The stem often starts with Identify, so you are picking one key fact out of a list.

02

State, Give, Identify, Name (1-2 marks)

Recall a fact, term, example or short definition, or pick out the key information from a figure, table or piece of code. No reasoning is needed.

03

Describe (2-4 marks)

Give an account of what something is, what happens, or the steps in a process. The statements should link together and build up the picture, but you do not need to say why.

04

Explain (2-4 marks)

Make a point and then justify it: say why or how it is true. 'State and justify' questions work the same way: a choice first, then the reason for it.

05

Compare and evaluate (2-4 marks)

Compare: show how two things are alike or different, talking about both. Evaluate and Assess: weigh up strengths and weaknesses, then reach a judgement that you support with reasons.

06

Discuss (6 marks)

Pick out the issue in the question, explore its different sides and develop each idea with reasoning. Any bullet points in the question show areas you are expected to cover.

07

Methods, tables and code: show every step

Carry out a method rather than write prose: convert between binary, denary and hexadecimal, work out file sizes, complete truth and trace tables, write logic statements, follow or complete algorithms, and write or fix pseudocode and Python code.
